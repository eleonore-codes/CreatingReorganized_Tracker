# CreatingReorganized — Side-Hustle Hero's Journey Tracker

Track your side-hustle journey from first spark to first income — with Prof. Dr. E as your mentor. Log milestones across 6 hero levels, map all 24 CreatingReorganized episodes, reflect on wins, reframe failures, and ship your first idea this week. Built for FOM students. Works for any degree. Your future-proof business starts here. 🚀

---

## What This App Does

This is a single-file, offline-ready HTML tracker built for students who want to explore entrepreneurship alongside their degree — without overwhelm, without a business plan, and without quitting their studies.

It follows the **CreatingReorganized Hero's Journey**: a 6-level progression from your first idea all the way to your first income, guided by the real episodes and psychology-backed frameworks of Prof. Dr. Eleonore Soei-Winkels.

---

## Features

- **6 Hero Levels** — Spark → Shield → Pivot → Flow → Tribe → Owner, each with milestones, reflection prompts, and a mapped module reference
- **All 24 Episodes Linked** — every CreatingReorganized episode mapped to its level, with direct links to the show page
- **💡 Ideation Lab** — pick your hustle format, complete a one-page business canvas, and commit to shipping something real this week
- **📓 Reflection Engine** — self-chosen goals, weekly wins, reframed failures, and a Vision of Life statement
- **🧠 Un-AI-able Skill Badges** — log what you did today that an AI couldn't
- **💰 Income Log** — track every euro from your side-hustle; first entry at Level 6 unlocks the Future-Proof Captain badge + confetti
- **⚙ Settings** — enter your degree (BSc, MSc, MBA, PhD and more), your exact module names, and map them to each level so the app reflects your actual curriculum
- **🛠 Founder's Toolbox** — Prof. Dr. E's affiliate partner stack with honest notes on which tools have programmes and which don't
- **✨ CreatingReorganized Button** — a motivational quote from Prof. Dr. E pulled from across all 24 episodes, whenever you hit a wall
- **LocalStorage** — everything saves automatically in your browser, no account needed

---

## How to Use

1. Open the tracker on [postdoctransformation.ac-page.com/creatingreorganized_tracker](https://postdoctransformation.ac-page.com/creatingreorganized_tracker)
2. Go to **⚙ Settings** and enter your degree, university, and module names
3. Start at **Level 1: Spark** and work through the milestones
4. Use the **💡 Ideation Lab** to sketch and ship your first side-hustle idea
5. Log your reflections weekly and your income when it arrives
6. Listen to the linked CreatingReorganized episodes as you level up
